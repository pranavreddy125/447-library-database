## Vision and Scope Statement

### Vision Statement — Why Are We Developing This?

Our vision is to create an organized and user-friendly library database system that makes it easy to manage books, members, and borrowing records while allowing library information to be accessed quickly and accurately. The system will use PostgreSQL as its relational database platform, hosted and managed through Supabase. Pranav has worked with this platform before and will help Karim and the team.

### Mission Statement

Our mission is to develop a reliable database system that stores and manages information about books, library members, checkouts, returns, and availability. The system will reduce manual record-keeping, maintain accurate data, and make everyday library operations more efficient.

### Scope Statement — What Are We Including?

The project will include the following parts of a relational database system for a small library:

- **Collection and membership:** Manage books, digital media, magazines, client profiles, membership types, and account status.
- **Circulation and rules:** Track checkouts, returns, due dates, availability, transaction timestamps, borrowing limits and restrictions, and late fees.
- **Reservations and notifications:** Allow clients to reserve loaned items and provide notices for upcoming due dates, overdue items, and available reservations.
- **Users and interfaces:** Consider preliminary staff and client interfaces. Staff can manage items and accounts and process loans; clients can search the catalog, reserve items, and view loan status.
- **Reports and queries:** Support fine calculations, item availability, overdue items, borrowing trends, client activity, and at least one detailed management report.
- **Term deliverables:** Produce an ER model, a normalized relational schema that reduces duplicate data and keeps information consistent, a PostgreSQL implementation with constraints and triggers, realistic sample data consisting of at least 20 books, 20 digital media items, 20 magazines, and 50 client records, and demonstration queries and reports.
- **Part 1 boundary:** This document defines the planned system. Detailed modeling, schema design, SQL implementation, data population, and demonstration will be completed in later project phases.
